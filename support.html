Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common CROPLAND questions.

How can I sell my crops on CROPLAND?

Log in to your farmer account, open your dashboard, and use the available crop listing option to add your crop details, quantity, expected price and harvest information.

How can I check my orders?

Open the My Orders page from your dashboard to view the orders available in your account. Order status depends on the current order workflow.

What should I do if a shopkeeper does not respond?

You can use the Messages page to communicate with the shopkeeper. If the issue continues, submit a support request with your order details.

How can I check my earnings?

Open the Farmer Earnings page to view the earnings information available in your account.

How do I update my profile?

Open your Farmer Profile page and use the available profile editing options.

What if my payment is delayed?

Keep your order details and payment reference ready. Submit a support request explaining the issue. Do not share your password, OTP or PIN.

Can I change my crop quantity or harvest date?

Open your crop planning or crop registration page and check the available editing options. If editing is unavailable, contact support.

Is CROPLAND support available for technical issues?

Yes, you can describe website errors, login issues, or other technical problems using the support form below. Actual support handling will require a connected backend.
